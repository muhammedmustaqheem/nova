Technical specifications.

What runs, in order, on every case. Evidence is opened read-only, and repairs are always written as separate files.

IntakeAcquire & sealSHA-256 primary seal, SHA-1 / MD5 for FTK, EnCase and Autopsy manifests, CRC-32 read guard. Genesis block of a hash-chained audit log. O_RDONLY
RecoverUndelete & carveFAT directory entries marked 0xE5, plus signature carving of unallocated space for JPEG, PNG, PDF, ZIP, SQLite, PE and text.
ReconstructFragment joins256-bin byte-histogram cosine similarity, boundary entropy continuity and format match score every orphan pair. Best chains are verified by decoding before they are kept.
AssessIntegrity & bucketsHeader 25 · Footer 20 · Decoder 35 · Size ratio 10 · Entropy 10. Scores map to four recoverability buckets.
DetectAnti-forensicsRansom notes, high-entropy cipher regions, zero-fill wipe runs, and executables disguised as documents.
ReportSealed deliverablesPDF and self-hashed JSON manifest, CSV inventory, full audit trail, and a post-analysis re-hash proving the evidence was not modified.
